CHANGELOG
=========

v1.1.00
-------
New permission "New Request_onBehalf" (create tickets on behalf of others): its holders see every active service and
can send a request for another person, who becomes the requester; the ticket history says who created it.
Teams can have a default assignee: new tickets of the team's ticket types are assigned to that person straight away.
Notifications: a new ticket for a type whose teams have no members goes to the administrators; notification centre
entries are written before emails; failures are written to Gibbon's log.

v1.0.00
-------
First release. Service catalog and request forms per ticket type (with child and class pickers for parents),
teams, tickets with comments / internal notes / history, workflow per ticket type copied from a default workflow,
automation scripts on stage entry (Record absence in Attendance, Unenrol from classes), notification policy per
person and per ticket (notification centre and email), personal and shared saved filters.
